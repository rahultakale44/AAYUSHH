## 🎯 Quick Demo (3 Minutes)

### 1. Create Emergency (60 seconds)
1. Click **"Senior Citizen"**
2. Select language: **English**
3. Fill form:
   - Name: **Test Patient**
   - Age: **75**
   - Allergies: **Penicillin**
   - Contact: **+91-9876543210**
   - Symptoms: **"chest pain and difficulty breathing"**
4. Click **"Review"** → **"Confirm"**
5. ✅ See RED urgency badge + Session ID

### 2. Emergency Response (60 seconds)
1. Click **"Home"** → **"Emergency Response"**
2. Find your session
3. Click **"Acknowledge"** → **"Assign Transport"**
4. ✅ Status updates

### 3. Transport & Hospital (60 seconds)
1. Click **"Home"** → **"Transport Partner"**
2. Click **"Accept Transport"** → **"Start Route"**
3. Click **"Home"** → **"Hospital Operations"**
4. Click **"Admit Patient"** → **"Complete"**
5. ✅ Full workflow done!
